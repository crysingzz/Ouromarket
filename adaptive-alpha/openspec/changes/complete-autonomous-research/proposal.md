## Why
The existing laboratory only mutates deterministic templates. It cannot discover literature, generate code, or recover queued research after a worker interruption.

## What Changes
- Add persistent research campaigns, external evidence ingestion, OpenAI structured generation and an explicit Ouroboros task adapter.
- Execute a restricted, bounded Python strategy language against progressively available history, with no native execution of generated source.
- Add immutable imported datasets, scientific diagnostics, knowledge links, campaign artifacts and operator UI.
- Record integration readiness separately from implementation and empirical acceptance.

## Impact
Adds autonomous-research capability. Existing demo-v1 experiments and risk policies retain their original meaning. Generated strategies remain research artifacts until independent admission requirements are satisfied.
