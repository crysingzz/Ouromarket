# Design

`ResearchSpec` gains an immutable `MechanismDescriptor` and a bounded set of `EvidenceClaim` values. A claim names one evidence version and one exact packet passage, states a short proposition and declares whether that passage supports or contradicts the proposition used by the specification. Every claim anchor must also be present in the spec's citation set. Evidence-backed specs require at least one claim; repeated claim identities and contradiction text without a contradicting claim fail validation.

WorkOrder registration revalidates the packet and all claim anchors in the same transaction. It stores one claim version per WorkOrder. The record identity covers the WorkOrder, spec hash, statement, relation and anchor. Graph edges record the asserted relation from passage to claim and the specification link from claim to WorkOrder. `anchor_verified=true` means the byte-level passage binding passed; `semantic_status=researcher_asserted` and `verified=false` prevent that fact from being misrepresented as independent scientific verification.

The mechanism descriptor uses a closed family and input vocabulary plus bounded horizons and premise text. The exact fingerprint covers the normalized premise and all structured fields; the family fingerprint covers the family, inputs and direction. Normalization applies Unicode NFKC, case folding and collapsed word tokens so capitalization and punctuation do not evade identity. An immutable candidate-mechanism index avoids a finite recent-candidate search window. The novelty diagnostic reports exact and family matches from retained candidates. A novel campaign rejects an exact match from outside its current campaign lineage as `DUPLICATE_MECHANISM`; iterations inside one campaign remain explicit lineage so engineering fixes can proceed. A replication campaign may test another implementation, but retains the matching candidate IDs as mechanism lineage. AST duplicate detection remains an independent code-level control.
